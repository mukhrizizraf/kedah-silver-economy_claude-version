What the dashboard needs to work

The score here runs on sample logic. These sheets list the data we would collect, check and keep updated before it can be used for real.

11linked workbook sheets
Example data only
The workbook holds linked example profiles, needs, districts, providers, contacts and referrals. The phone numbers and aid amounts are made up. Real records go in only after consent, data rules and source checks are approved.

Person profile

Who is asking for help, where they live and how they like to be contacted.

person_id · age · sex · district · village · language · consent_statusKept by the field team · updated at the first visit
02

Needs assessment

One person can have more than one need, each with how urgent it is and the evidence.

assessment_id · person_id · need_code · severity · frequency · urgencyKept by the assessor · updated at each review
03

Household and eligibility

Income, family support, current aid and the rules used to decide who qualifies.

household_size · monthly_income · carer_present · benefit_statusKept by the welfare partner · updated every 3 months
04

Providers and services

Who can help, what they offer, where they work and whether they have space.

provider_id · organisation · service_code · district · capacity · statusKept by the network lead · updated every month
05

Referrals and outcomes

Each referral, reply, waiting time and result, so we can see if the matching worked.

referral_id · person_id · need_code · provider_id · outcome · closed_atKept by the coordinator · updated after every referral
06

Lists and scoring

Fixed lists and open weights that turn checked data into the score.

need_code · district_adj · severity_weight · capacity_weightKept by the research team · each change gets a version number
