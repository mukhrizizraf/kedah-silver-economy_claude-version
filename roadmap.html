Design workshop · Phase 3

Who takes part

15–20 people from these groups. They look at the findings, agree priorities, roles, funding, rules and referral steps, then build the model together.

  • MAIK
  • LZNK
  • PERKIM
  • JKM
  • Elderly care NGOs
  • Takaful
  • Health providers
  • Religious leaders
  • Academics
  • Older people's representatives

Expert review · Phase 4

How the panel scores the model

A panel of 10 people from MAIK, LZNK, JKM, NGOs, community leaders, takaful providers and universities scores each part of the model and writes comments.

  • Relevant
  • Workable
  • Practical
  • Agencies ready
I-CVIscore for each item
S-CVI/Aveaverage for the whole model

Small trial

What the trial checks

After the expert review, we try parts of the model on a small scale, mainly how agencies refer people to each other.

  • Does it work
  • Do people accept it
  • Are roles clear
  • What gets in the way
  • What to improve

Main risk: timing (medium)Busy people may miss workshops. So we invite more people, and early, keep a backup from each group, stay flexible on dates and bring key agencies in as partners. Technical and budget risks are low.

Budget

How the RM30,000 is spent

Most of it pays the project team for nine months. The rest pays for travel, two workshops, tokens for participants, printing, copyright filing and the 3% RMC fee.
